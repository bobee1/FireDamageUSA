How fire risk differs across the country

A fire damage call in Maine looks different from one in Arizona. Here are four national patterns from federal data, and the states where each one stands out.

PatternStates where it stands outWhat it means for cleanup
Most FEMA wildfire assistance since 2000California, Texas, Washington, Oklahoma, OregonSmoke, ash and odor in homes the fire never reached
Highest fire death risk (USFA, 2023)Hawaii, Washington, DC, Alaska, West Virginia, OklahomaFast-moving house and wildland fires; heavy structural damage
Most homes heated with fuel oilMaine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Connecticut, AlaskaOily puff-back soot that smears if wiped with water
Most homes heated with woodVermont, Maine, Montana, New Mexico, IdahoChimney and stove fires, creosote and smoke in wood interiors

Sources: OpenFEMA fire declarations 2000 to November 2026; USFA State Fire Death Risk (2023); Census ACS 2024 house heating fuel.
