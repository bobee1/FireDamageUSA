Before you call: 5 safety steps after a fire

  1. Wait for the all-clear. The American Red Cross advises staying out of a fire-damaged home until local fire officials say it is safe to go back in. Floors, ceilings and stairs can be weaker than they look.
  2. Leave the utilities alone. Do not switch power, gas or water back on yourself. The fire department or the utility company will tell you when that is safe.
  3. Get the fire report number. The U.S. Fire Administration suggests writing down the incident report number from the responding fire department. Your insurer will ask for it.
  4. Call your insurance company. Report the loss right away and ask what your policy covers for temporary housing, board-up and cleaning.
  5. Photograph everything. Once you are allowed in, take photos and video of each room, the damaged items and any soot on walls and ceilings, before cleanup starts.

If your family needs a place to stay, food or clothing tonight, contact your local American Red Cross chapter. Their disaster teams help people after home fires every day.
