Accessibility Statement

Anyone recovering from a fire should be able to use this site, including people who rely on screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control.

Document: SootMend Accessibility Statement · Effective November 19, 2026 · Maintained by Anjam Javed

Our target

SootMend works to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). WCAG is the most widely used measure of whether a website works for people with disabilities.

Features on every page

  • A "Skip to content" link as the first item on the page.
  • A logical heading order, so screen reader users can move from section to section.
  • Text colors with a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 against their background.
  • A clear focus outline on links, buttons and form fields for keyboard users.
  • Alt text that describes each meaningful photo and illustration.
  • Labeled form fields, with status messages read out by screen readers.
  • Layouts that fit phones and tablets without sideways scrolling, and that honor reduced-motion settings.
  • Phone numbers set up as tap-to-call links.

Known limits

We link to outside sites, such as government agencies and fire safety groups. We do not control those sites, and some may not meet the same standard. If a page on our own site gives you trouble, please let us know.

Ask for help or report a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, call 1-800-555-0199 and we will go over the information with you by phone. You can also report the issue through the contact form. Include the page address and a short description of what went wrong. We aim to reply within 5 business days and fix confirmed problems quickly.

Testing and review

New pages are tested with a keyboard and automated checks before launch, and this statement is reviewed at least once a year. Our editorial policy explains how the rest of the site is reviewed.
