Fire damage questions in Wichita

Do you cover all of Wichita?

Yes. SootMend takes fire and smoke damage calls from across Wichita and Sedgwick County, including College Hill, Riverside, Delano, Old Town and Crown Heights, and ZIP codes such as 67202, 67203, 67208, 67211.

How fast should cleanup start after a fire in Wichita?

Within a day or two. The EPA says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold. Soot left on surfaces in Wichita homes keeps staining and eating into them.

Does insurance cover fire damage in Wichita?

In most cases, yes. A standard Wichita home or renters policy pays for sudden fire and smoke damage up to its limits. Ask early about living expenses, and contact the Kansas Department of Insurance if a claim stalls.

Why is fire risk higher in Kansas than average?

Older homes, space heaters, rural distances and wind-driven grass fires all play a part. Working smoke alarms, safe heater use and yearly furnace service make the biggest difference.
